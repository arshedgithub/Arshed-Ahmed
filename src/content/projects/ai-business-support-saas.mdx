## The problem

Businesses looking for grants and funding need clear guidance, and the platform delivers it through multiple AI chatbots. To run as a SaaS, it needed reliable billing: one-time payments, subscriptions, plan changes with fair proration, and refunds. The team also needed to see what was happening with payments without digging through the Stripe dashboard.

## Key technical decisions

{/* TODO: confirm: the decisions come from the CV; the reasons given for them are my draft. */}

- **Webhooks as the source of truth.** Subscription and payment state is updated from Stripe webhook events rather than trusting the client.
- **Automated proration** when customers change plans, so charges stay correct without manual work.
- **Refunds handled in the same payments layer**, so one-time and subscription payments follow the same flow.
- **Jest unit and integration tests** covering payment and API logic to reduce release regressions.
- **A real-time payments dashboard** giving the team live visibility into transactions, subscriptions and refunds.
