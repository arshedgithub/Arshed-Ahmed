## The problem

Job seekers often send CVs that applicant tracking systems (ATS) parse badly, and they rarely get to practise interviews with useful feedback. This platform tackles both: it helps candidates build ATS-friendly CVs tailored to a job description, then lets them rehearse with a voice-based mock interview.

## Architecture

A Next.js and TypeScript app handles the UI and server logic. Resume generation, parsing and job-description optimisation call a Groq-hosted LLM. In the mock interview, Whisper turns the candidate's spoken answers into text, the LLM generates feedback, and text-to-speech reads questions and feedback aloud. Data is stored in PostgreSQL through Prisma. {/* TODO: confirm the text-to-speech provider. */}

## Key technical decisions

- **Groq LLM** for fast responses, so resume suggestions and interview feedback feel immediate.
- **Whisper speech-to-text** so candidates answer out loud, as they would in a real interview.
- **Job-description optimisation** that compares a CV against a target role before generating changes.
- **PostgreSQL and Prisma** for typed access to users, resumes and interview sessions.
